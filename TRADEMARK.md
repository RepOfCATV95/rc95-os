# RC95-OS trademark and brand policy

The RC95-OS source code is free software under [GNU GPL 3.0](COPYING). However, logos and wordmarks of the Republic of CATV 95 as well as its old names are protected by CATV 95 Republic's Posting Rules (https://www.facebook.com/notes/856968265077440/) which is still in effect as of September 17, 2010. As such, publishing binaries using such old and new names of the Republic of CATV 95 are wholly FORBIDDEN, even with written permission.

## What you may do without asking permission

- **Redistribute an official build unchanged.** Only official sources for download are [Releases](https://github.com/RepOfCATV95/rc95-os/releases). Unofficial sources are not supported.

## What needs permission

- **Shipping modified code under the name** Rename the resulting sources.

## Other people's marks

The logos, product and company names and assets belong to their respective owners. ReactOS is a trademark by its owner and the Republic of CATV 95 does not claim endorsement by that project.

## Enforcement and permission

The Republic of CATV 95 enforces this policy under its existing Posting Rules, even old names must NOT be used without permission.

Also, foreign ownership of artwork made by the Republic of CATV 95 is FORBIDDEN under both 1987 Philippine Constitution and CATV 95 Posting Rules.
